What changes for requestors?

  • Patient matching shifts from statewide QE fan-out to patient lookup with Verato/sMPI support.
  • Document discovery becomes manifest search against a centralized repository index.
  • Document retrieval becomes direct API access to repository-held CCDs and clinical documents.
